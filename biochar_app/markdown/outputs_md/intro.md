<p>The Fruita Biochar Project is completing its fourth year in 2026. Please explore the website. If you encounter a problem or have a suggestion for improvement, please email <a href="mailto:nelson.gerald.c@gmail.com">nelson.gerald.c@gmail.com</a>.</p>
<p>The big news is the addition of a Draft Report tab presenting the study's findings. We will revise the report as the remaining 2026 data are finalized and comments are received. Please send comments and suggestions to <a href="mailto:info@biocharresearch.org">info@biocharresearch.org</a>.</p>
<p>This introduction provides an overview of the project and website. Here's what you can expect:</p>
<ul>
<li><p>A concise explanation of what biochar is and why it may be valuable in western Colorado and other drought-prone regions.</p></li>
<li><p>An overview of the biochar experiment at the Colorado State University research station outside Fruita, Colorado.</p></li>
</ul>
<p>Two categories of data are presented: continuous monitoring and laboratory-based results.</p>
<ul>
<li><p>The Continuous Monitoring Results menu presents sensor data as <a class="tab-link" data-tab="main-tab" href="#">Interactive Plots</a> and <a class="tab-link" data-tab="summary-tab" href="#">Summary Statistics</a>.</p></li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li><p>The Lab-Based Results menu presents <a class="tab-link" data-tab="biochar-analysis-tab" href="#">Biochar Material Characterization</a>; forage quality (<a class="tab-link" data-tab="nir-tab" href="#">Pasture Quality Metrics</a>); forage quantity (<a class="tab-link" data-tab="biomass-field-tab" href="#">Biomass Samples and Fertilizer Recommendations</a>); <a class="tab-link" data-tab="soilbio-tab" href="#">Biological Health</a>; and <a class="tab-link" data-tab="soilchem-tab" href="#">Soil Chemistry</a>.</p>
<p>The remaining tabs provide:</p></li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li><p>Technical details about data collection.</p></li>
<li><p>Bulk downloads of Campbell Scientific sensor data, local weather variables, fertilizer applications, irrigation water, soil results, and forage quantity and quality.</p></li>
<li><p>A glossary.</p></li>
<li><p>Acknowledgments of everyone who has made this project possible.</p></li>
