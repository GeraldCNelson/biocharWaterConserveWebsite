}</style></head><h2 id="directions-for-use-of-the-options-in-the-interactive-plots-tab">Directions for use of the options in the Interactive Plots tab</h2>
<p>Use this page to explore the continuously collected data. For the most part, the data are displayed for a year or a subset of a year and a single variable at a time, at various resolutions, from 15 minutes to the growing season. In the top plot, you can see data either from a single logger with sensors at different depths, or from three loggers at one depth. The bottom plot shows ratios – S1/S2 (the two west strips with S1 having biochar) and S3/S4 (the two east strips with S3 having biochar) – for the chosen variable at the chosen depth.</p>
<p>The control panel (left side) has the following choices:</p>
<ul>
<li><p>Toggle between metric and US units</p></li>
<li><p>Use the dropdowns to choose year, variable, strip, logger location, and depth.</p></li>
<li><p>Click "Update Plots" to refresh the Raw (top) and Ratio (bottom) plots after changing a drop down.</p></li>
<li><p>Drag a rectangle on the top plot to zoom in on a smaller time window. The ratio plot will automatically be synced.</p></li>
<li><p>Use the "Download Data" and "Download Raw and Ratio Plots" menus to export what you see.</p></li>
</ul>
<p>Item-specific help is available with a mouse over ⓘ.</p>
